Our principles

Free of the subscription model. Free of tracking.

  • No subscriptionsPay once and the app is yours. No monthly fees, no renewal reminders, no features held hostage.
  • No data collectionWe don't collect, sell or share your personal information. We don't run servers that could hold it.
  • No ads or trackingNo advertising, analytics or tracking code inside our apps. What you do in them is your business.
  • Your data stays yoursYour information lives on your own devices and in your own iCloud account, under your control.
